Background image

A wide 16:9 picture (1920×1080 or bigger) looks best. Darken it a little so the board stays easy to read; the live preview on the right updates as you go.

Folder option: put a file named background.jpg (or .png / .webp) in the images folder and reload. A picture chosen here takes priority over the folder file.

Music & sound effects

Click Replace… to use your own audio file (mp3, m4a, wav) for any sound. It's saved in this browser, so you only do it once. Reset goes back to the built-in sound. The slider sets that sound's volume; drag it all the way left to turn it off.

Folder option: file names to use

Instead of clicking Replace, you can drop files into the sounds folder with these exact names (any of .mp3, .m4a, .wav, .ogg), then click Rescan sounds folder. Sounds picked with Replace take priority over folder files.

Before the party

  1. Put your survey results in Setup & Questions (or in survey-data.js) and set the team names.
  2. Connect the laptop to the TV/projector as an extended display (not mirrored). Set the Mac's sound output to the TV or speaker.
  3. Click Open audience screen, drag that window onto the TV, then click inside it once to make it full screen. All sound plays from this operator window.
  4. Optional: Print host sheet so the host can read the questions from paper.
  5. Optional: in Look & Sound, pick your own background picture and swap in your own music or sound effects.

Running a main round

  1. Start the round from the strip at the top. The big screen shows the round intro, then the board.
  2. Face-off: the host reads the question. When a player buzzes in, press Q (left team) or P (right team). If their answer is on the board, click it (or press 1–8). If it isn't, press X for a single X.
  3. The face-off winner decides to play or pass. Click “… plays” for whichever team will play.
  4. Play: click each correct answer. Press X for a strike. Three strikes moves to the steal.
  5. Steal: the other team gets one guess. If it's on the board, click it and they win the bank. If not, click Steal failed (or X) and the playing team keeps the bank.
  6. If the playing team finds every answer, they win the bank automatically.
  7. Afterwards, Reveal remaining shows the answers nobody got (no points). Then start the next round.

Scoring

  • Every answer revealed during the round, including the face-off, adds points × multiplier to the bank.
  • The team that wins the round gets the whole bank. A successful steal also adds the stolen answer to the bank.
  • The first team to the winning score (default 300) wins. If you set it to 0, the highest score after the last round wins.
  • Sudden death (tiebreaker): only the #1 answer is on the board. Whoever names it wins the round (triple points). Use Award bank to give it to them.
  • Made a mistake? Use Undo (⌘Z) or edit the scores in the sidebar.

Bonus round

  1. Pick two players from the winning team. Player 2 leaves the room so they can't hear. Player 1 gets 20 seconds to answer the 5 questions.
  2. Type what they say in each box, or click a survey answer chip to fill in the text and points. Click ✗ 0 if it isn't on the list.
  3. Press Space to start or pause the timer.
  4. Use Reveal on each row (answer first, then points), or Reveal all for the whole sequence.
  5. Switch to Player 2. Turn on Hide Player 1's answers, give them 25 seconds, and press D to play the duplicate sound if they repeat an answer.
  6. The total hitting the goal (default 200) sets off the win celebration.
