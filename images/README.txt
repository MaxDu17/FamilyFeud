SWAPPING THE BACKGROUND
=======================

Easiest way: in the operator console, open the "Look & Sound" tab and click
"Choose image...". It's remembered in this browser.

Folder way: put a picture in THIS folder named

  background.jpg   (or background.png / background.webp)

and reload the operator page.

A wide 16:9 image (1920x1080 or larger) looks best. Use the "Darken" slider
in the Look & Sound tab so the board stays easy to read, and turn the snow
and string lights on or off there too.
